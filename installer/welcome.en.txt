Local Agent AI Station — early preview

A Windows control center for local models, agents and GPU monitoring.
The installer and documentation support English, Russian and Ukrainian.
The application interface currently uses Russian.

Installs to C:\Program Files (administrator rights once); the app itself runs without them.
Models, agents and GPU drivers are installed separately. Startup is off by default.
No GPU service is installed automatically. GPU driver mode switching requires
the optional helper and compatible hardware; it remains an experimental feature.

To update or uninstall, choose Exit in Station's tray menu first.
Settings remain in %LOCALAPPDATA%\LocalAgentAIStation after uninstall.
